I am a researcher with a background in the Social Sciences and more than eight years of experience applying research to different contexts, from social and anthropological studies to markets, consumers, digital products and customer experiences.

My work is grounded in understanding people within their social, cultural and market contexts. I use qualitative and quantitative research to investigate behaviours, needs and expectations, examining how people relate to products, services and organisations. This perspective allows me to approach business challenges from different angles, connecting human behaviour, market dynamics and organisational needs.
