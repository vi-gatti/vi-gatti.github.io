03

AI in my research practice

I integrate AI-assisted workflows throughout the research lifecycle, using Microsoft Copilot with Claude-based skills and workflows, and Dovetail AI, within approved enterprise environments. AI is part of the research process, not a standalone productivity tool.

  1. 01

    Research planning & intelligence

    Structuring research briefs and plans, reviewing discussion guides, conducting desk research and benchmarking, and analysing complex internal and external information to explore hypotheses and research directions.

  2. 02

    Research Operations & knowledge management

    Automated spreadsheets for participant recruitment, organised research materials and thematic knowledge repositories. Previous studies were synthesised into accessible knowledge bases, so teams could build on existing evidence and avoid duplicated research.

  3. 03

    Interview analysis & synthesis

    Recordings and transcripts analysed with Dovetail AI for tagging and thematic grouping, then cross-checked against my own field notes, observations and contextual interpretation to identify patterns, contradictions and relationships across interviews and sources.

  4. 04

    Insights & strategic communication

    Support for comprehensive reports, executive summaries and short insight videos, translating complex findings into accessible, actionable knowledge for stakeholders.

  5. 05

    AI-assisted prototyping & visualisation

    Figma Make used to build navigable prototypes, explore concepts, support user testing and give visual form to research insights, helping stakeholders discuss possible solutions.

Human-led, AI-assisted

AI supports information processing, analytical exploration and operational efficiency. Research strategy, methodological decisions, contextual interpretation and validation remain human-led.

All AI use involving corporate information followed approved enterprise technology and data governance requirements.
