I also introduced secondary school students to scientific research and research methodology, developing experience in investigation, knowledge organisation and communication. In 2017, I also graduated in Education (Pedagogy) from Cruzeiro do Sul University.

  3. 03

    Applied Research

    Markets, consumers, products & services

    I brought this foundation into applied research across the public sector, a research institute, consulting and corporate environments. My work has included market and consumer studies, innovation projects, digital products and customer experience, across financial services, telecommunications and retail.
