01

Project overview

Strategic and UX research for a major Brazilian telecommunications provider, as part of a digital transformation that brought its residential and mobile self-service apps into a single experience.

Over six months, the research connected customer evidence with product, business and technology decisions, from discovery to the first phase of implementation.

The integration was one concrete step in a wider effort to reduce fragmentation across services, systems and customer touchpoints.

Industry
Telecommunications

02

Context

The company had grown over decades through different products, systems and organisational structures. Residential and mobile customers managed their services in separate apps, even when they were clients of both.

Fragmentation was also internal. Different systems and teams made it hard to build a single view of the customer and to communicate consistently across channels.

03

The challenge

Customers should not need to understand how the company is organised in order to solve their problems.

The task was not simply to combine two applications. It was to understand where fragmented journeys created friction, and what a more integrated service would need to offer residential, mobile and combined-service customers.

Three layers were at stake: how customers accessed and managed their services, the consistency of the experience across channels, and the integration of areas, products and systems behind it.

04

Research objectives

The research aimed to:

  1. Understand how different customer profiles used residential and mobile services.

  2. Map needs and pain points in the existing digital journeys.

  3. Examine how competing providers organised their digital services.

  4. Identify which customer groups would be most affected by the integration.

  5. Evaluate existing and proposed functionalities with users.

05

Research approach

A mixed-methods approach in three moments, run alongside the integration work.

  1. 01

    Research and discovery

    Customer and stakeholder interviews, questionnaires, NPS and TNPS analysis, market and competitor research and journey analysis. The results were synthesised into three research-based personas.

  2. 02

    Strategic alignment

    Findings were discussed with UX and design, product, technology and leadership, connecting customer evidence to the priorities of the integration.

  3. 03

    Implementation follow-up

    Moderated and unmoderated usability tests continued as new flows were proposed, and weekly cross-functional meetings followed the first integration deliveries.

06

Key findings

  1. Distinct patterns of behaviour and satisfaction between residential and mobile customers.

  2. Three personas made differences in needs and pain points visible to the teams, including customers with combined services.

  3. Integration had to go beyond consolidating features.

    Access, product management, billing, benefits and the move between self-service and human support all shaped the experience.

  4. The market and competitive analysis placed these findings within the wider digital service landscape.

07

From research to decisions

The findings informed the integration of the two self-service experiences and wider discussions on a more connected digital ecosystem.

Directions discussed with the teams:

  1. Unified access to residential and mobile services.
  2. More centralised management of products.
  3. Simpler billing and service information.
  4. Better-connected benefits and services.
  5. More consistent support across digital and human channels.

08

Outcomes

The research contributed to bringing two separate self-service apps into one experience, simplifying how customers accessed and managed residential and mobile services.

I stayed involved through the first implementation phase, until the first integration deliveries. The integration was part of a wider transformation that continued after the project.

09

My contribution

I worked as the UX Researcher alongside the UX and design manager, on the research and alignment of the integration.

I conducted customer and stakeholder research, analysed journeys across residential and mobile services, developed the personas, ran the usability tests and brought the evidence into discussions with product, design and technology.

I also took part in the weekly cross-functional follow-up, staying involved as the first phase of the integration moved into delivery.
