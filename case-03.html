Connecting B2B Customer Intelligence to Commercial Strategy

Building a scalable customer understanding framework for corporate insurance.

01

Business context & challenge

Strategic research for the corporate insurance offer of a major Brazilian bank’s wholesale division, across five insurance lines.

Companies had a close relationship with the bank, but insurance was offered reactively and was not connected to that relationship. Commercial officers knew their clients, but not always who dealt with insurance in each company, or what each product required.

The commercial team needed to understand how each company decides to buy protection, and who takes part in that decision, across a large client portfolio.

Four transversal customer archetypes were incorporated into the bank’s 2026 commercial method.

Industry
Financial services and corporate insurance
Organisation
Major Brazilian bank, wholesale division
Research
87 client interviews, 5 insurance lines
Period
2024–2026

02

Research programme

Progressive, qualitative-led research: client interviews by insurance line, interviews with commercial officers, insurance specialists and operations, market analysis and, later, data analysis with the data team. The categories were not decided upfront; each study revealed new dimensions of the B2B decision.

03

Key insights

The person who holds the banking relationship is not necessarily the one who buys insurance, and the seats around a decision can pull in different directions. Company behaviour, more than size, shaped how protection was bought.

For each company, the commercial team needed to know:

  1. Who holds decision power.
  2. Who carries operational responsibility.
  3. Who knows the product.
  4. Who influences the choice.
  5. Who has their own interests at stake.
  6. Who to involve at each stage.

04

Customer intelligence framework

Findings were organised as layers of customer knowledge. Product-specific profiles kept the depth of each market; the archetypes gave a shared first reading, applicable to any product.

Commercial officers did not need to master every insurance product. They needed to spot opportunities, ask the right questions and bring in the specialist, who received a better-qualified context.

05

Commercial application & impact

Each insurance consultant supported 15 to 20 commercial officers, each with a client portfolio. Personalisation had to start before the consultant joined the conversation, so the client base had to be classified.

Applying the archetypes to the client base:

  1. DoneData and spreadsheet analysis with the data team.
  2. DoneCriteria to identify the transversal archetypes.
  3. DoneInitial classification of the client portfolio.
  4. PilotedTests and pilots to evaluate the criteria in practice.
  5. ProposedAutomated lead identification and future AI integration, still under discussion.

Impact at three levels:

  • Strategic

    Four transversal archetypes incorporated into the 2026 commercial method.

    Adopted

  • Tactical

    Support materials, criteria, sales arguments and an approach guide.

    Delivered

  • Operational

    Initial portfolio classification, data analysis and pilots.

    In progress, July 2026

06

My contribution

I translated a complex organisational reality into a decision model the business could use.

I conducted research across the insurance lines, synthesised it into product profiles and transversal archetypes, and helped build their commercial application. I worked with the data team on the classification criteria and took part in the pilots. I left the bank in July 2026, while the application at scale was still in progress.
